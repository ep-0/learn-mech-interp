## Why this article exists

Patching an activation and measuring the change in output is a mediation analysis, and the noising and denoising variants correspond to different mediation estimands. Naming them explains why the two directions can disagree.

## What this article will cover

- Mediators, total effect, and the decomposition into direct and indirect
- Controlled versus natural direct effects
- The interventions that identify each effect, and their assumptions
- Interaction between mediators, and why effects need not add up
- Noising and denoising as different mediation questions
