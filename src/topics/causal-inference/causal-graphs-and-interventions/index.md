## Why this article exists

Activation patching is an intervention on a computational graph, and the entire argument that interpretability findings are causal rather than correlational is borrowed from this framework.

## What this article will cover

- Directed acyclic graphs, parents, and structural equations
- Conditioning versus intervening, and the do-operator
- Interventional distributions, and why they need a graph
- Paths, colliders, and d-separation
- A neural network read as a causal graph over its activations
