## Why this article exists

Eigenstructure is how we reason about repeated or accumulated linear action, and it is the machinery behind the singular value decomposition, covariance analysis, and the copying behavior read off an OV circuit.

## What this article will cover

- The eigenvalue equation and the characteristic polynomial
- Eigenspaces, multiplicity, and defective matrices
- Diagonalization as a change of basis that makes a map coordinate-wise
- The spectral theorem for symmetric matrices
- Complex eigenvalues as rotation, and what they mean for real maps
