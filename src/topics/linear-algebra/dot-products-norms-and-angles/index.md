## Why this article exists

Attention scores are dot products. Feature strength is a dot product. Similarity between two learned directions is a normalized dot product. The single operation carries most of the geometric reasoning in the curriculum.

## What this article will cover

- The dot product, its algebraic and geometric definitions, and why they agree
- Euclidean and $L^p$ norms, unit vectors, and normalization
- The angle between vectors and cosine similarity
- The Cauchy-Schwarz inequality and what it bounds
- Orthogonality as zero alignment, and why it is the useful special case
