## Why this article exists

Ablating a feature means projecting it out. Reading a feature's strength means projecting onto it. Direct logit attribution, ablation steering, and concept erasure are all built on the same decomposition.

## What this article will cover

- Orthogonal decomposition of a vector relative to a direction
- Projection matrices onto a subspace, and the complementary projection away from it
- Idempotence, symmetry, and what characterizes an orthogonal projection
- Gram-Schmidt and orthonormalizing a set of directions
- Oblique projections, and why the choice of inner product matters
