## Why this article exists

Autoregressive language modeling factors a sequence distribution into conditionals, and probe calibration, detection thresholds, and base-rate arguments about monitoring are all Bayes' rule applied carefully.

## What this article will cover

- Conditional probability and the chain rule of probability
- Independence, conditional independence, and how conditioning creates dependence
- Bayes' rule, priors, likelihoods, and posteriors
- Base rates, and why a good detector can still be mostly wrong
- The autoregressive factorization of a sequence distribution
