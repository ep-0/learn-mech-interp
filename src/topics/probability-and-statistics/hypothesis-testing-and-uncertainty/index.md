## Why this article exists

Interpretability results are usually differences in a metric measured on a finite set of prompts, and a large fraction of the field's contested claims are contested because that difference was not accompanied by an interval.

## What this article will cover

- Sampling distributions, standard errors, and confidence intervals
- Null hypotheses, p-values, and what significance does not mean
- Effect size versus statistical significance
- Multiple comparisons, and correction when screening many components
- Bootstrap and permutation tests for metrics without closed forms
