## Why this article exists

Steering vectors are differences of expectations. Probe training minimizes an expected loss. Layer normalization subtracts a mean and divides by a standard deviation. These moments are the arithmetic underneath.

## What this article will cover

- Expectation, linearity, and the law of the unconscious statistician
- Variance, standard deviation, and the bias-variance identity
- Covariance, correlation, and what correlation does not imply
- Independence versus zero correlation
- Law of large numbers and the central limit theorem
