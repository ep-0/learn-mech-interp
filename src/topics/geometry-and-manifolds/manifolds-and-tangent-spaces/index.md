## Why this article exists

When activations concentrate on a curved low-dimensional set, the linear-direction vocabulary stops applying exactly, and the replacement is a manifold with a tangent space at each point.

## What this article will cover

- Manifolds as locally Euclidean sets, with examples such as the sphere and the circle
- Charts, coordinates, and smooth transitions between them
- Tangent vectors and the tangent space at a point
- Embedded manifolds in $\mathbb{R}^n$, and the manifold hypothesis
- Why a linear direction is a first-order approximation to a curved one
