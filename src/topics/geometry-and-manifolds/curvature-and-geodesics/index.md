## Why this article exists

Manifold steering moves an activation along a learned curved path rather than adding a fixed vector, and the difference between those two moves is exactly the difference between a geodesic and a straight line.

## What this article will cover

- Riemannian metrics and measuring length on a curved space
- Geodesics as locally shortest paths
- Curvature, and how it makes parallel paths diverge or converge
- Exponential and logarithmic maps between a manifold and its tangent space
- Why linear extrapolation leaves a curved manifold
