## Why this article exists

Integrated gradients accumulate a gradient along a straight path from a baseline to an input, which is a line integral. Continuous probability distributions need the same machinery to define expectations.

## What this article will cover

- The definite integral as accumulated change, and Riemann sums
- The fundamental theorem of calculus
- Multivariable integration and integration over a region
- Line integrals along a parameterized path
- Path independence for conservative fields, and why integrated gradients has a completeness property
