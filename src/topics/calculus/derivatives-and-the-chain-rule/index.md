## Why this article exists

Backpropagation is the chain rule applied to a composition a hundred layers deep, and gradient-based attribution methods are the same rule read as a statement about influence. This is the refresher the rest of the calculus articles build on.

## What this article will cover

- The derivative as a limit, and as the slope of the best local linear fit
- Rules for sums, products, quotients, exponentials, and logarithms
- The chain rule for composed functions, and how it iterates
- Differentiability, continuity, and functions such as ReLU that fail at a point
- Higher derivatives and what they say about local shape
